## Example image

`examples/milky_way/input.jpeg` is the byte-for-byte user-supplied photograph
originally named `FishEye_MilkyWay_no_trails.jpeg`. Its SHA-256 is recorded in
`examples/milky_way/baseline.json`. Original photographer, capture date,
location and redistribution licence have not been documented. It and its
annotated derivatives are retained here as research test materials;
check rights before public redistribution. No authorship of the photograph
is asserted.

The BSD 3-Clause License does not grant rights in the underlying example,
MMTO or other third-party images. Generated overlays and reports may incorporate
those images; their original terms still apply.

The MMTO figures in this repository use colour FITS data made available by the
MMT Observatory. Publications using those data should acknowledge the MMT
Observatory. See the live archive at
[skycam.mmto.arizona.edu](https://skycam.mmto.arizona.edu/skycam/archive/).

The reference annotation and association tables were generated by the
point-star solver. The display-name cache contains SIMBAD identifiers and
aliases retrieved on 13 September 2026. SIMBAD is operated at CDS, Strasbourg:
[SIMBAD](https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/simbad/). These aliases are used solely
for labels and do not supply astrometric positions to the fit.

## Catalogues and dependencies

The bundled catalogue derives from Tycho-2 and Hipparcos, distributed by
CDS/VizieR. See [data/README.md](data/README.md) for catalogue references,
selection, epoch handling and source terms. The project software licence does
not replace catalogue or data-provider terms. Retain those attributions in
derived work.

The scientific model follows
[Barghini et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201935580).
The blind pattern bootstrap uses [ESA tetra3](https://github.com/esa/tetra3),
pinned to commit `f9fa2eb9a32a5efc529e2d86f0b59f35b1e9028d`, including its
bundled default pattern database. Other software dependencies are declared
in `pyproject.toml` and resolved in `uv.lock`; their upstream licences apply.
